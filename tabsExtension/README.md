# Tabs Explorer (99 Tabs)

A simple extension dedicated to putting all tabs in a single place with easy search and mute toggles.

Essential for the tab hoarder that has 10+ tabs browser window with 5 browser windows open.

Did a random youtube video started playing when you restore your 100+ tabs and have no clue where is at? Just look for the playing icon or toggle the is playing filter with !isPlaying.

Wanna be ashamed that you have had that "I will work on next" project tab that you haven't even viewed in over 6 months? Just hover over the clock to see when was the last time you viewed a tab. (When the tab is initially open is on the wishlist)

Wanna go directly to a tab but dont remember the where you left it. Just click on it. The tab and the window will become focused!
